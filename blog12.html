AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning

Introduction

These terms are related but not the same.

AI

Broad concept of smart machines.

Machine Learning

AI subset that learns from data.

Deep Learning

Uses neural networks for complex tasks.

Example

  • AI → Self-driving car
  • ML → Traffic prediction
  • DL → Image recognition

Conclusion

Understanding these differences is essential in modern tech.
