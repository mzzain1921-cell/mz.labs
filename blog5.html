Keyword Research Process

What is Keyword Research?

Keyword research is finding the exact words people type into search engines.

Types of Keywords

1. Short-Tail Keywords

Example: “SEO”

2. Long-Tail Keywords

Example: “SEO for frontend developer portfolio”

Long-tail keywords convert better

Keyword Research Process

  1. Understand your audience
  2. Find keyword ideas
  3. Check search volume
  4. Analyze competition
  5. Select best keywords

Tools for Keyword Research

  • Google Keyword Planner
  • Ubersuggest
  • Ahrefs
  • SEMrush

Example

Instead of targeting “developer”, target:

“Frontend developer in Pakistan”

Conclusion

Keyword research decides whether your content succeeds or fails. It is the heart of SEO strategy.
