What is Artificial Intelligence?

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines to think, learn, and make decisions like humans.

Types of AI

1. Narrow AI

Designed for one task

Example: Chatbots

2. General AI

Human-like intelligence (future concept).

Real-Life Examples

  • Google Search
  • Voice assistants
  • Recommendation systems

Conclusion

AI is shaping the future of technology and innovation.
